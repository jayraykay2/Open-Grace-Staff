Our Mission

Open Grace is committed to empowering adults with developmental disabilities and their families through person-centered, culturally responsive Coordinated Family Support Services.

We focus on dignity, stability, and independence while strengthening natural family supports and community connections.

What we do

Core service areas

CFS is flexible and built around each person's IPP goals. Most families need help in more than one area at once, so we work across all four.

Stability & Planning

  • Backup providers and contingency plans for individuals living with aging caregivers
  • Long-term planning: future housing, conservatorship questions, and Special Needs Trust referrals
  • Benefits support for SSI, IHSS, Medi-Cal, and CalFresh applications and renewals

Care Coordination

  • Service navigation across agencies and providers
  • Transportation coordination
  • Appointment scheduling and follow-through, so nothing falls through the cracks

Independence & Quality of Life

  • Skill building and independence-focused training
  • Daily routines, money management, and step-by-step goals
  • Meaningful participation in daily and community living, including job readiness and DOR referrals

Family & Caregiving

  • Family training and guidance toward natural supports
  • Education related to each individual's diagnosis and identified needs
  • Caregiver support, including AAC and communication tools for non-verbal individuals
Who we serve

Is CFS right for your family?

Coordinated Family Support is a Regional Center service. It complements services like IHSS and ABA rather than replacing them.

  • Adults age 18 and older eligible for Regional Center services
  • Individuals choosing to remain in the family home
  • Families seeking structured, short-term coordination and support

If CFS may be right for you, ask your Regional Center Service Coordinator about a referral to Open Grace, or take our short pre-screening.

Getting started

How it begins

Referral

Your IRC Service Coordinator refers your family to Open Grace. You can also ask for CFS at your next IPP meeting.

Introduction

Director Joshua Kennedy calls or texts your family within 48 hours to introduce Open Grace and set up an intake conversation at a time that works for you.

Services begin

Once CFS is authorized in your IPP, your coordinator starts working alongside your family toward the goals you choose.

For Service Coordinators

Referring a family to Open Grace

  1. Confirm CFS authorization in the IPP and note the areas of need.
  2. Contact Joshua Kennedy by phone or email with the referral and any background that helps us start well.
  3. We reach the family within 48 hours to introduce ourselves and schedule intake.
  4. Services and documentation follow, with IPP-aligned contact logs, progress notes, and quarterly reports.

Common questions

Frequently asked questions

What is Coordinated Family Support (CFS)?

CFS is a Regional Center service for adults with developmental disabilities who live at home with family. It focuses on independence, caregiver support, and connecting families to the services and resources they need, tailored to each person's IPP goals.

Who qualifies for CFS?

The individual must be 18 or older, have a developmental disability, live at home with family or caregivers, be a Regional Center consumer such as with Inland Regional Center (IRC), and have CFS authorized in their current IPP.

Does CFS replace IHSS, ABA, or other services?

No. CFS helps your family get approved for services you may not have yet, keeps existing services working together, and fills gaps other services do not cover.

Can you support families who primarily speak Spanish?

Yes. We provide culturally responsive services in English and Spanish and help families communicate with providers and agencies.

Can a coordinator come to appointments with us?

Yes. When appropriate, your coordinator can join appointments to help explain information, advocate for your loved one, and make sure your concerns are heard.

Can CFS help with non-verbal individuals or complex needs?

Yes. We help obtain communication tools such as AAC devices and PECS, coordinate specialized services, and train caregivers so support meets the person where they are.

How do we get started?

Ask your IRC Service Coordinator for a referral to Open Grace, or contact us directly and we will help you take the next step.
